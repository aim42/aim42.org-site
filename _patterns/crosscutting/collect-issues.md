---

You should constantly watch out for issues (problems and risks), especially during [analysis](/patterns/analyze/) activities like [Stakeholder Interview](/patterns/stakeholder-interview/) and others.

The artifact (physical collection) is the [Issue List](/patterns/issue-list/).

Regularly match these issues to your [collection of possible improvements](/patterns/collect-opportunities-for-improvement/).
